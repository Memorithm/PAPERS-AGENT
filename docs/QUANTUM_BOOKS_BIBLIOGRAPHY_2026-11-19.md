## Important edition and access distinctions

- Numilog identifiers `9782759822867` and `9782759823352` refer to **paper-book ISBNs** on the EDP website. Official PDF ebook ISBNs are `9782759822850` for II and `9782759823369` for III.
- The publisher's 2019 tome III should not be confused with the first printing catalogued by the BnF in **2017** (`9782759821075`).
- A third-party mirror resembling the complete tome III was found, but its redistribution authorization could not be established. It was **not** selected as the source.
- No PDF file or full-text extract was committed. The full-text access and training flags are **false** and are not modified by a citation or by purchase of an individual ebook.

## How to use in Memorithm

PAPERS can discover and search the titles/authors/tags through its `PaperRegistry` and record provenance. The only content in `papers_registry.json` is bibliographic metadata written for this catalogue. This does **not** permit an unreviewed training pipeline to ingest source files.

A separate, documented rights clearance must precede any full-text extraction, RAG/embedding storage, redistribution or SML-GENIUS training; training and TEST roles remain unassigned. Memorithm/RemoteOps remains the sole control plane for any future on-premises jobs.
